NORTHWIND ANALYTICS - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (SAMPLE DATA, FICTIONAL COMPANY)
Version 3.2, effective 1 April 2026

1. WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00; meetings should be scheduled within this window.

2. LEAVE POLICY
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.
Sick leave: 12 days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.
Parental leave: 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver, fully paid.
Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance, except sick leave.

3. REMOTE WORK
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's approval.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.
The company provides a one-time home-office allowance of 500 USD for equipment.

4. TRAVEL AND EXPENSES
Economy class is mandatory for flights shorter than 6 hours; business class may be booked for longer flights.
Hotel costs are capped at 220 USD per night in tier-one cities and 150 USD per night in tier-two cities.
The daily meal allowance during travel is 60 USD.
Expense claims must be filed within 30 days of the expense with itemised receipts attached.
Claims above 1,000 USD need approval from the finance controller.

5. INFORMATION SECURITY
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 180 days.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and all customer-data systems.
Confidential documents must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
Security incidents must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery.

5.1 DATA CLASSIFICATION
Public: may be shared freely. Internal: employees only. Confidential: need-to-know, encrypted at rest.
Restricted: customer personal data and financial records; access is logged and reviewed quarterly.

6. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December.
Ratings use a 5-point scale. Employees rated 4 or above are eligible for the annual bonus pool.
The annual bonus target is 10% of base salary for individual contributors and 15% for managers.

7. CODE OF CONDUCT
Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated and may lead to dismissal.
Gifts from vendors above 50 USD in value must be declared to the compliance team.
Conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing to your manager and compliance.
